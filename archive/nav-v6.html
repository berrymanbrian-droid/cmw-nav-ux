Core Website Challenge

This site presents a unique navigation challenge:

  • It contains a massive amount of information — courses, calculators, research databases, community features, media, and personal saved data all competing for attention in the navigation.
  • It needs to work for people across a very wide range of ages and technical abilities — from first-time hobbyists who've never heard of a UMF, to advanced ceramicists running complex test series daily.

The navigation must guide without overwhelming. New users need clear entry points and wayfinding. Power users need fast access without friction. Both need to coexist in the same interface.

User Stories

New User

As a brand new user of the website, I want to be able to clearly see all of the things that the site can provide and do, so that I can understand the value of a membership and find the right starting point for my experience level.

  • Browse available courses and understand what each one covers
  • Explore free content (Glaze Anatomy, Free Tools) without signing up
  • Understand what membership tier is right for me
  • Find the "Where do I start?" guide
  • Watch or listen to free content (For Flux Sake, YouTube)
  • See what the community looks like (Fired!)

CMW Student

As a student of CMW, I want to be able to easily find information and resources for the specific class that I have signed up for, so that I can complete labs, create and post results, and communicate with Matt.

As a student of CMW, I want to be able to see other classes that might be available to me to sign up for in the future, so that I can plan my learning path and continue growing.

As a student of CMW, I want to be able to find helpful resources and Q&A content, so that I can answer questions I have while working without having to wait for Matt to respond or for the next hangout.

  • Access my enrolled course materials, labs, and discussions
  • Post lab results and view classmates' work in Commons
  • Check the hangout calendar and sign up for live sessions
  • Use the Glaze Calculator to work through course exercises
  • Search for answers to specific glaze problems (Troubleshooting, Concepts)
  • Browse recipes and reference materials while working in the studio
  • Save glazes and clay bodies to My Work for future reference
  • See what other courses are available and what's open for enrollment

Experienced User

As an experienced user who has previously taken CMW courses, I know my way around the basic and advanced tools. I'm coming back to fix specific issues, run more advanced tests, or dig into the deeper research and information that CMW provides.

  • Jump straight to the Glaze Calculator or Clay Body Calculator
  • Use Fix a Flaw or Troubleshooting to solve a specific problem
  • Design and run advanced test series
  • Dig into research collections (Test Test Test, Stull Maps, Stains)
  • Compare glazes or clay bodies side by side
  • Read The Journal for deeper analysis and arguments
  • Access saved data (My Glazes, My Test Reports, My Workbooks)
  • Share results and participate in the Fired! community

Keep it Simple User

As a user who may be an amazing potter but isn't interested in hunting around the site, I have a few key things I regularly use and I want to get in and out quickly. I'm unlikely to leave feedback if something frustrates me — I'll just stop using it. The interface needs to stay out of my way.

  • Access my most-used tools immediately (calculator, saved glazes)
  • Use a "simple view" option on complex tools like the calculator
  • Find answers quickly via search or AI without navigating menus
  • Have a personalized dashboard or shortcuts for frequent actions
  • Avoid being overwhelmed by features or options I don't use
  • Get to my saved recipes and data in as few clicks as possible

Design consideration: Look for ways to offer simplified views, progressive disclosure, and clear defaults. These users won't tell us when something is wrong — they'll just leave. The AI search bar and "Where do I start?" CTAs are key tools for this persona.

Stakeholder / Business

As the business owner, I need the site to clearly communicate the valuable work that CMW does. I need to convert visitors into subscribers, demonstrate the value of upgrading membership tiers, and keep the site organized enough that users don't get frustrated and cancel.

  • Clearly communicate what CMW offers and why it's worth paying for
  • Surface membership upgrade paths naturally (tier pills, locked features with tier labels)
  • Show users what they're missing without being aggressive or paywalling the experience
  • Keep navigation clear and logical so users don't churn from frustration
  • Make it easy for new visitors to understand the value before signing up
  • Highlight courses with open enrollment to drive sign-ups
  • Reduce support burden by making answers self-service (AI search, Troubleshooting, FAQ)
  • Track which features drive upgrades and which cause drop-off

Design consideration: The navigation itself is a sales tool. Every locked feature pill is a soft upsell. The "Where do I start?" flows and AI search reduce friction that leads to cancellation. The hierarchy should make the breadth of content visible without overwhelming — demonstrating value at every level.
